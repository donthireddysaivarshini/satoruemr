Why it shows a contact, not the username: In CHT, every user account must be linked to a contact (person). The report's "Submitted by" header always comes from that linked contact's name — it's how CHT identifies who submitted. There's no mode where it shows the raw username.
What happened in your case: When varshini's user was created in admin, her account got linked to Vijayamma's contact (contact_id: 019ffcb0-...). So even though the form's fields.user.name = varshini, the report header uses the linked contact = Vijayamma.
The only way to show "Submitted by varshini": varshini's user must point to her own contact. That means creating a varshini contact (like you created NIMS/Jayasri/etc.) and updating org.couchdb.user:varshini.contact_id to it. Then the flow is exactly what you expect:
- varshini submits → "Submitted by varshini"
- any other user submits → their own name
